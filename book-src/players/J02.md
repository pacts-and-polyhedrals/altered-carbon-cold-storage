# Sleeves and Resleeving

Organic, clone and synthetic bodies form an unequal economy. Some people maintain preferred bodies and secure backups; others accept what is available. A new sleeve can change physical capabilities, sensations, clinical needs and strangers' assumptions without determining your values.

Your current sleeve and four to seven verified earlier sleeves are on your character sheet; a few disputed files may be listed separately as unverified records. The old names describe earlier parts of your life, not five NPCs the GM may use to control you. Ask about accommodations and known maintenance needs. The adventure does not treat a particular body type, disability or gender as a moral failing.

Resleeving and damage use the table's Altered Carbon system. The separate Fray rule is an agreed adventure addition, explained in the player Fray page. A read-only memory replay is not automatically another resleeve.
