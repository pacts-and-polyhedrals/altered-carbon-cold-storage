# The Fray Agreement

**Optional house rule for this adventure. It does not replace the Altered Carbon system's Ego or resleeving rules.**

Your character has four through seven verified previous sleeves and now a fifth, sixth, seventh or eighth non-clone body. Some legacy archival records are disputed. Coercive memory work has left old experiences poorly integrated. Fray measures the acute conflict reopened tonight, not a real-world diagnosis and not the supposed inferiority of any body type.

With your agreement, start at **Fray 2 on sleeve #5, 3 on #6, 4 on #7, or 5 on #8**, on a 0-6 track. You may instead use the same Fray 3 baseline for everyone, or opt out entirely. There are five major scheduled continuity challenges, not a check every time someone mentions an old life. At a challenge, use Composure in realspace or Discipline in Virtuality: D0 at Fray 0-3, D1 at 4-5. Success holds the track; failure adds one. Instead, you may accept an echo: take +1 without rolling, describe what the former self wants, and receive a truthful extra detail or a small concession. The GM cannot use an echo to order your actions.

A deliberately dangerous recall or root-link may offer an extra advantage for **+2 Fray and raw 1d6 Ego loss**, subject to the system's applicable reductions. The GM must explain that cost and benefit before you choose. No essential clue requires it. A genuinely new emergency resleeve adds +2 Fray once, plus applicable core consequences; viewing a recording does not.

You have two free grounding opportunities: one at a safe regrouping and one before the final decision. Name a present-tense value and have another participant acknowledge it; each reduces Fray by one, once. These do not restore Ego. Normal clinical or resource-based Ego recovery remains separate.

## At Fray 6, you choose

**Integration:** return to 3. An old value becomes part of the present identity and one attachment changes in a lasting way you define. Facts and skills remain. The change matters in your ending.

**Partition:** return to 4; take raw 1d6 Ego loss with applicable reductions. A chosen emotional memory is sealed until future care. Its factual information remains available, and you cannot use voluntary overreach again tonight.

**Shelter:** once tonight, return to 4 after a protected interval. The adversary gains time, raising Heat by one or creating a stated custody/evacuation concession if Heat is already full. You keep participating through a safe channel or agreed temporary role; you do not sit out the session.

The GM cannot choose an intimate loss for you, force betrayal or demand violence. A professional loyalty, ambition or chosen obligation can carry the consequence instead. Ego zero is a separate core-system crisis and uses the table's prior agreement about continued play, care or a reserve character.

You can veil a description, decline a personal topic or opt out of this mechanic. Using a safety boundary never causes a resource penalty. Your present identity, bodily consent and response to the past remain yours.


**Important:** Fray is a scenario-specific house rule, not a medical diagnosis or an automatic official penalty for changing bodies. Permanent Ego damage, Trauma/Personality Frag Baggage, and Psychosurgery are separate Core mechanics. The GM will explain any actual starting Baggage penalty and whether earlier Ego consequences were already included before requesting your choice. Treatment is available but never compulsory.
