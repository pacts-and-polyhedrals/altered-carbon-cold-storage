# Relationship Archive: GM Navigation

**GM ONLY.** The original archive retains 160 links: each of the forty original archive records has an enemy, rival, ally and loved-one connection. Two claims of extra bodies are disputed, and six verified early preludes were added without inventing new relationship ties. The twenty recurring contacts can occupy different roles across different lifetimes. Do not treat that as an error or automatically as evidence of an impostor.

Use [[G25|the Contact Ensemble]] for present agendas and verification paths, [[G26|Eight Investigators]] for selected-PC spotlights and the original relationship dashboard to advance reveal states. Familiar, suspected and confirmed correspond to familiarity, an identity claim and independent verification.

Promote a manageable selection during a one-shot. The remaining relationships support later sessions, alternate leads and a richer history; they are not mandatory scenes.
