# What Is a Person?

A cortical stack preserves a person's continuing digital identity, commonly called DHF. A sleeve is the body through which that identity experiences the world. Bodies, documents, memories and social recognition do not always agree.

Your character has a continuous history across several sleeves. That does not mean every recalled detail is reliable or that every old attachment must remain unchanged. An unfamiliar body can have needs, abilities and habits of its own without making you someone else's property.

In this investigation, distinguish familiarity from proof. A gesture may remind you of someone. A stranger may claim an old name. Verification requires a record or witness independent of the claim. You are allowed to ask for it, including from people who seem kind.
