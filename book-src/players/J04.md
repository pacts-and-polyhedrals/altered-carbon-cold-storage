# Bay City and the Rainline

Bay City climbs vertically from flood-shadow and service queues to protected wealth above the weather. The Rainline is your corner of it: old industrial blocks, stacked walkways, clinics, religious shelters, relay towers and markets built into infrastructure that outlived its original purpose.

The city remembers your absence unevenly. A building may have vanished while a small shop remains. A former colleague may have changed bodies and politics several times. Look for human-scale details as well as conspiracies: who is waiting, who is working, who can open a door and who has to ask permission.

Three powerful civic patrons appear throughout public advertising: Dr Adria Sen, Magistrate Severin Holt and Director Oren Thale. They are known as the Three Benefactors. Their influence reaches clinics, legal administration and transit. People speak of them with gratitude, resentment or both. What that influence means for your investigation remains to be established.
