# Player Briefing: The Faces We Left Behind

**Bay City, Earth. The year is 2384. The last year you remember is 2274.**

You wake in a body you did not choose. Four to seven earlier sleeves lie behind you in the history of one continuing life. Now 110 years have passed without you. People you knew may still be alive, but no familiar face can guarantee who is inside it.

You were part of an investigation into Project Palimpsest. Your last secure memories end before you learned the whole truth. You carry a protected piece of the old case, not an obligation to finish whatever your previous self intended.

This is a roleplay-heavy investigation. Ask questions, compare records, decide who deserves trust and make plans with consequences. Violence is possible, not mandatory. Sensible investigation obtains essential clues; rolls usually decide the costs, advantages and complications of getting them.

Choose a name you want used tonight and an anchor: a person, value, object or commitment that helps you distinguish what you choose now from what you once expected. You may reshape how a historical loved-one relationship is expressed with your GM. No NPC is entitled to intimacy, obedience or control over your body because of your past.

Your present identity belongs to you. Your memories can be evidence without being commands.
