# Everyday Life in an Unfamiliar Body

You are allowed to care about a cup of coffee, a chair, a familiar song or the way rain sounds. Small choices can establish who you are now more effectively than an answer to a philosophical test.

Ask for what your current sleeve needs. You do not have to perform discomfort for the group. Describe something useful or unexpectedly pleasant as well as something difficult. A character can have mixed feelings about a body without the game resolving those feelings for them.

Old habits can be kept, changed or allowed to pass. Someone who knew you before can offer information, not a veto over the name or life you choose tonight.
