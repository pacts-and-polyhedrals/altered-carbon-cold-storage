# Artificial Intelligence

Artificial intelligences operate services, remember customers, manage virtual spaces and maintain systems that human institutions rely on. People disagree about their rights, obligations and independence.

An AI may remember a human visitor across several changes of sleeve. That makes it a potentially useful witness, not an automatically perfect one. Ask what it directly observed, what records it retained and what it learned from somebody else.

Marlowe, the manager of Anansi House, is one such possible witness. The old hotel was associated with your former investigation. Whether to trust a particular record or relationship is a question you can test rather than settle by the speaker's origin alone.
