# The Lost 110 Years

Your last secure memories are from 2274. It is now 2384. You were not consciously living through the entire interval. Do not invent an extra century of imprisonment memories unless the GM explicitly establishes a different individual event.

Other people did live through those years. They may have waited, moved on, compromised, changed beliefs or built families. A surviving bond can be real without returning to its former shape.

Your verified earlier sleeves remain part of your history. The present investigation may challenge particular details, but it does not declare your whole life fabricated. Ask what a record actually contradicts before surrendering every memory to doubt.
