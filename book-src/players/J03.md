# Death, Continuity and Choice

Sleeve death, a missing stack and unrecoverable personal death are different situations. When danger arises, ask what is threatened: the current body, the stack, a known backup, access to that backup or legal control over it.

Recovery is not equally available to everyone. Money, infrastructure, law and other people's help determine what can actually be done. A historical backup on a character sheet is not a promise of instant access after a century of absence; its present status may need to be checked.

No one should assume consent to resurrection, treatment or copying merely because a person is recoverable. A witness can have rights while stored. A person can choose a current life without erasing every earlier one.
