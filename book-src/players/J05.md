# The Aerium and Wealth

Above the Rainline, wealth buys preferred sleeves, protected records, legal representation and distance from the consequences of ordinary scarcity. Exceptionally long-lived elites are often called Meths. Their security depends on institutions, not literal invulnerability.

A powerful patron can be helpful and still impose obligations. Ask who owns a replacement sleeve, what a contract permits and which promise survives a change of body. Sabine Kade's history offers one perspective on elite society, not an automatic answer about everyone in it.

The central inequality tonight is not simply who can live longer. It is who gets to decide what a continuing life is allowed to be.
