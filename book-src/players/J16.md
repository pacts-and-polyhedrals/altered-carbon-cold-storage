# Organisations You May Meet

The **Continuity Foundation** publicly supplies restoration care and sleeves. The **Protectorate Continuity Office** administers stack evidence and state continuity matters. **Sable Needle Corporation** operates relay and transmission infrastructure.

The **Blue Lotus Combine** participates in the illegal sleeve and identity economy. The **Quiet Archive** preserves witnessed testimony and consent records. The **Chainbreakers** organise against concentrated control over people's continuity.

Membership is not a complete moral description. Ask what the particular person wants, what they can actually provide and what they will not do. The same organisation can contain sincere helpers, compromised professionals and people using its authority for private ends.
