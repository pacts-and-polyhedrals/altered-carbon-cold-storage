# Faith, Testimony and Consent

People disagree about resurrection, the meaning of continuity and whether a stored person should be awakened to testify. The adventure does not require agreement on a religion or a universal answer to those questions.

The Quiet Archive preserves written testimony and witnessed records outside compulsory spin-up. Its custodians may protect both people who choose resleeving and people who refuse it. Ask what the person actually authorised before assuming that an investigation overrides their wishes.

An existing deposition, a living witness and an independent record can answer a question without making an unwilling person relive it. Faith is part of the city's ordinary life, not a shorthand for ignorance or villainy.
