# Distant Worlds and Older Histories

Some of your archived lives took you beyond Bay City. Harlan's World, colonial service, customs work and encounters with the traces of Elder technology appear in those histories. They shape what your character values and recognises.

Tonight's case is local. You do not need detailed setting trivia, an interplanetary journey or knowledge of television protagonists to solve it. An older professional experience may offer a useful comparison, but the decisive evidence is available here.

Tell the GM which earlier lifetime interests you. It can provide an echo or a question without taking the whole group away from the present investigation.
