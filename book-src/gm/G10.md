# SC03: The Dead City They Remember

**30 / 45 / 60 minutes across all selected leads.** Purpose: let investigation be a choice between people with needs, not a sequence of search rolls. Play one, two or three leads according to the schedule. Cast: one leading contact and one relationship contact per visited location.

## Arrival

> The city has built another city on top of the one you remember. A memorial occupies the corner where you bought breakfast. The canal has moved behind a concrete wall. An advertisement tells you that your second life should feel like coming home.
>
> Beneath it, three smiling patrons stand in white coats, judicial black and transit blue. Someone has left flowers at the advertisement's maintenance hatch.

A passer-by can identify the Three Benefactors without a check. They are powerful civic patrons, generally assumed to be Meths with broad institutional influence. The passer-by knows no secret. Ask each PC to name one small place they remember. Give two a surviving trace and one a genuine loss. Do not spend the whole scene cataloguing vanished lives.

## The route choice

Tom Arlen can appear in person for Nia, or call through Serin's burner. He says: "Paper, cargo or relays. Ash has the paper. Saint has the cargo. Orr has the relays." Give [[H14|the Rainline lead card]]. All three places can help. None is the secret correct order.

**The Quiet Archive** asks who can speak for the dead. It offers records, consent evidence and a protected witness. Run [[G11]]. **Blue Lotus** asks what a person will sell to survive. It offers delivery manifests, a compromised contact and an escape route. Run [[G12]]. **Sable Needle** asks whether reliable evidence is worth losing a career. It offers relay provenance, maintenance access and practical civilian safeguards. Run [[G13]].

Players may split. Alternate short conversations every few minutes, but keep a shared communication channel and do not double the table-time allowance. There is no private solo ambush waiting for the smaller group. The core mystery needs collaboration, not six simultaneous side campaigns.

## The first relationship contradiction

Use the first relevant historical contact the party meets. The contact knows a private habit but has also changed something meaningful: a marriage, religious interpretation, professional allegiance or promise. State the contradiction plainly. "I kept the letters. I did not keep waiting." Or, "I signed the order after I swore I would stop it. Both things are true."

This is scheduled Fray pulse 2, once for each participating PC for whom the encounter or received testimony raises a continuity conflict. PCs without a personal tie can react to the shared record showing a group decision differently from their memory. Do not apply one pulse per visited lead. If no street leads are visited, stage this pulse at Anansi's first verified contradiction.

## How the city responds to the sleeves

An officer initially addresses Cass's police chassis as a colleague. A clerk assumes Sabine's non-clone labour sleeve has no authority to sign for an archive. A door recognises Imani's body as somebody else's paid account. Let the PCs accept, correct or exploit the assumption. A successful social choice can save a check or reduce Heat; it is not a demand to roleplay discrimination for entertainment. Veil unwanted treatment and state the practical obstacle instead.

## A street incident, only when useful

A clinic intake worker has refused a woman a replacement sleeve because her identity certificate is marked "pending continuity reconciliation." She is alive and capable of speaking for herself. She asks for a witness, not rescue by force. A PC can sign a paper statement, contact Mira or use Bureaucracy D0 to obtain a temporary review. The result offers a concrete image of the stakes and an independent public receipt carrying a Benefactor-root code. It is a supporting Authority clue, not proof of artificial origin.

On a failure, the review is delayed; the worker gives the receipt anyway. Do not add a surprise child death or a fight. Skip this incident when the primary contact scenes already establish the city's human cost.

## Transition

When the selected leads are complete, a paper receipt folded into a spider shape arrives with a street courier: **"Your rooms remain yours. Anansi House."** Marlowe is not tracking their minds; Serin arranged the courier through the source they chose. The missing lead packets are waiting there with their named provenance. Move to [[G14|Anansi House]].
