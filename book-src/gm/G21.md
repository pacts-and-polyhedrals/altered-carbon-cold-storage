# SC06, Part Three: The Final Operation

**About 20 / 25 / 30 minutes within SC06.** This is an adventure-specific objective procedure, not a replacement for the core combat rules. Choose it for a conversational, cinematic finish. A group choosing full tactical combat uses that system instead, with the same evidence, switches and consequences; do not charge both procedures for one action.

## What the controls actually do

Give [[H13|the Control Diagram]] and [[H16|the Resolution Choices]]. The players can inspect everything before deciding.

**Evidence port:** makes a read-only, witness-signed case packet. It cannot copy an entire PC without a separate deliberate act. **Civic lease:** grants the three rulers' current administrative authority. It can be renewed, suspended or transferred through the audit path. **Life-safety bus:** keeps clinics, pumps and emergency routing operating under qualified staff. It is physically separate. **Clinical Palimpsest engine:** provides memory editing and interface maintenance. It can be quarantined, selectively retained for voluntary care or physically disabled. Destroying it does not immediately kill the three current residents; long-term maintenance becomes a medical problem, not an instant execution switch.

Backups outside the Spire are not all mapped. The party can record that uncertainty. Do not promise that killing local bodies destroys every copy or that seizing this room grants control of the whole Protectorate.

## Declare a plan in three parts

Ask for **an evidence outcome**, **an authority outcome** and **a care outcome**. For example: publish a redacted case to independent custodians; suspend the civic lease; preserve voluntary treatment under outside oversight. Or: bargain for protected witnesses; leave the lease temporarily intact under an enforceable audit; lock the coercive editing functions.

The named endings in [[G22|Resolutions]] are useful packages, not exclusive buttons. Hybrid plans are legal. A plan that publishes but leaves power intact is different from one that quietly transfers authority. Say back the group's intention before rolling anything.

## Three operation tracks

Each plan uses three short tracks, each requiring **two completed contributions**. A contribution is a specific achieved step, not a generic speech. **Proof** covers copying, redaction, witness signatures and reaching an outside custodian. **Control** covers the chosen lease change, binding settlement, engine quarantine or negotiated extraction. **People** covers staff handover, witness protection, safe departure and preservation of the reserve transport.

A previously secured advantage can complete one contribution: Ash's pledged custody completes an outside-recipient contribution; the successful redaction work completes the redaction contribution; a ruler's specific signed concession completes the corresponding Control contribution; the Last Broadcast overreach completes one Control contribution only. Count at most one pre-completed contribution per track, even if several advantages apply. Extra advantages grant credible access or avoid a consequence; they do not end the finale before everyone acts.

For a careful plan, all three tracks are achievable without killing anyone. Six PCs provide six opportunities per operation round, but each contribution must address a distinct unfinished task. Only **one attempt at each unfinished contribution per round** is allowed. Failed attempts can be retried in the next round using a new approach or the stated cost, not by cycling every PC through the same die roll. PCs whose task is already covered support a teammate, question a ruler, guard people or resolve a relationship.

## Resolution of an attempt

An uncontested step with the right access succeeds without a roll. A pressured or disputed step usually uses D0; an active adversary or a knowingly dangerous shortcut uses D1. State the benefit and one cost before rolling. Success marks the contribution. Failure offers a choice: leave it unfinished and try differently next round, or complete it at the announced cost. Appropriate costs include Heat +1, burning a contact's access credential, a witnessed custody obligation, loss of a clean escape route or raw 1d6 Ego loss for a **previously warned voluntary root-link**. Do not automatically assign Ego damage to every failed social action.

A cost cannot be paid by sacrificing another PC's memory, an unwilling civilian or an absent player's character. A player may refuse it. This keeps the procedure risky without letting dice author consent.

## Suggested tasks

| Track | First contribution | Second contribution | Appropriate approaches |
|---|---|---|---|
| Proof | Create a victim-redacted packet with all obtained pillars. | Place it with two independent custodians or one public channel plus a protected original. | Data Analysis, Investigation, Expression, actual witness arrangements. |
| Control: suspend/transfer | Obtain audit or maintenance access and specify the new lawful authority. | Apply the suspension/transfer and revoke delegated tokens. | Bureaucracy, Digital Networking, negotiated signatures. |
| Control: negotiated settlement | Record exact concessions and verification deadlines. | Place enforceable keys, funds or access rights with an independent custodian. | Diplomacy, Bureaucracy, contractual leverage. |
| Control: destroy/quarantine | Isolate the clinical engine from living minds and the life-safety bus. | Disable or seal the editing functions and record what remains. | Medicine, Engineering, Data Engineering. |
| Control: escape | Secure safe movement from the chamber. | Remove the party and their evidence from immediate custody. | Navigation, Stealth, contact commitments, custody negotiation. |
| People | Secure the independent operations handover or prevent civilian exposure from the chosen action. | Protect named witnesses and confirm the two reserve stacks' intact transit. | Engineering, Medicine, Diplomacy, leadership, practical escort. |

Using an alternative plan does not require completing every Control row. Pick the row matching their primary operation and fold any extra action into a concession or another round. A hybrid should have a manageable aim, not six different finales played at once.

## Renewal timing

Start at 0/4. Let every PC make one short contribution or support action. Then advance by one. The team normally has four rounds. Lena's maintenance token and a ruler-sponsored audit can each delay one advance, for a maximum of two extra rounds. Mark each delay used. The clock never advances because players ask you to clarify the controls.

At 2, Thale prepares his departure and offers a final logistics bargain. At 3, Asha orders the security personnel to separate armed participants from clinical staff; she can be persuaded to witness rather than suppress the operation. At 4, the lease renews if the chosen lease operation remains incomplete. A completed clinical quarantine, copied evidence or secured escape is not undone by that renewal.

## Partial results are playable results

**Proof complete, Control incomplete:** the world receives the case, but the rulers retain immediate authority. **Control complete, Proof incomplete:** the party changes power locally but faces a contested public account. **People incomplete:** name the specific unresolved harm: one clinic lacks a handover, one witness must flee, or reserve transport is held in review. Give the players a last choice of which person to protect; do not announce unspecified mass death.

If the group knowingly attacks the life-safety bus after being shown its function, casualties and emergency disruption are credible consequences. Signal them before the act, let the act be stopped, and never use civilian suffering as a surprise gotcha. Record who takes responsibility in the epilogue.

When the primary outcome is clear, stop resolving procedure. Move to consequences, not a fresh boss phase.
