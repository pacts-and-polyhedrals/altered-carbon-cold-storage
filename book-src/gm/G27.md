These numbers reproduce the supplied adventure profiles, not a claim of independent rulebook verification. ## Encounter use

**Ward retrieval:** one ADV02 lead and two ADV03 personnel for six PCs. Start behind cover with an offer to discuss custody. They do not all attack simultaneously before the party can act. End when a defensible transfer dispute is raised, their lead accepts terms, the PCs escape or the team takes unacceptable risk.

**Blue Lotus pressure:** one ADV02 extraction specialist and one ADV03 assistant, only if the optional Ren Ko confrontation becomes violent. They want the ledger and an unrecorded exit. A copied ledger means losing the original changes legal strength, not all clue access.

**Spire assault:** one ADV04 lead and two ADV03 guards. They hold an access point and protect staff. They withdraw to the council if the point becomes untenable. No automatic second wave appears just because the first wave lost.

**Virtual intrusion:** one ADV01 only after the PCs knowingly open the bidirectional diagnostic channel. It attempts to disrupt or obtain access, not annihilate an entire party. The physical disconnect and external replay remain genuine options.

These are objective-based encounter suggestions, not mathematically certified balance guarantees. Player system mastery, equipment and chosen tactics vary. For a new group, reduce supporting personnel and prioritise one teaching exchange followed by negotiation.

## The three Benefactors in conflict

Their primary defence is authority, staff and the opportunity to leave, not giant combat statistics. This package provides them as GM-only social NPCs with conservative ordinary organic-body resources; they are not bespoke boss stat blocks. If targeted in combat, use their imported ordinary-body values and the system's normal damage rules. Assign the ADV04 profile to a security operative, never silently to a physician who has been presented as unarmed.

Sen seeks cover and protects clinical residents. Holt orders witnessed custody and records the attack. Thale tries to reach a secure exit while retaining credentials. Vey tries to bargain for his usefulness. Destroying a local body has the consequences described in the council, not an automatic total victory.

## Equipment and action boundaries

Before a paid session, confirm that the original full importer produced each adversary's intended items and that the system sheet can roll them. The book-only updater intentionally does not rebuild actors or loadouts. If the world already contains modified actors, preserve those changes and inspect them rather than overwriting them with defaults.

The cinematic objective procedure is available when time matters. It determines whether the group gains access, copies evidence or protects people; it does not secretly apply weapon damage. When the table wants detailed combat, use the real combat engine and stop advancing objective rounds until the confrontation resolves. Renewal may be advanced at explicit, announced milestones, not once per weapon attack.

## Sleeve death and return to play

The retrieval teams prefer intact stacks. If a sleeve dies, state the condition of the stack and any known backup separately. A protected stack can be carried or connected to a safe communication unit; let the player continue contributing while a replacement is arranged. Do not make remote speech possible if the stack is actually destroyed or inaccessible.

An emergency resleeve is a meaningful risk: applicable core consequences plus the book's warned Fray increase. Serin has access to limited suitable spares, not unlimited instant perfect bodies. The group can choose evacuation or storage instead. Any reserve-character takeover requires the table's agreement and a clear update to who is active. Real Death is possible only through a situation whose stack danger has been clearly signalled and resolved in play, never as an offscreen penalty for an investigation roll.
