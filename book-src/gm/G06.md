# Evidence Docket and Revelation Web

## Evidence that can be trusted

A document proves what its provenance supports, not every claim printed on it. The withdrawal receipt proves which storage IDs moved. It does not prove the payer's motives. A body assay proves that a specific runtime occupies an organic clone; it does not prove that every person at the Foundation is an AI. The civic log proves administrative control, not that every service action was malicious.

Give the physical or read-only item when its scene says so. Mark it acquired on the docket. Players may copy, lodge or publish it. No later scene quietly deletes their copies. Loss of a local console changes access, not the existence of already secured evidence.

## Revelation redundancy

**Storage, not execution:** a PC recalls the arrest language; Ash has the sealed paper order; Asha can testify. **Palimpsest survived:** a PC recognises an accounting pattern; the trust deed traces legal succession; Lena explains the migration. **Consent was forged:** Imani remembers the actual gate command; the relay has a different timestamp from the accusation; Serin admits the exception sheet was reworded.

**The gods are embodied AIs:** a memory shows non-human runtime roots; independent manufacturing and origin records link those roots to the current bodies; Vey or the rulers can explain the interface. The complete claim is then tested directly at the core. These are separate sources, not three printouts of Marlowe's opinion.

## Recovering the case without six memory dives

The archive can be reconstructed from any four of the eight stored evidence shares. A read-only share is not a spun-up person. Serin holds reserve-share consent recorded in 2274, limited to releasing the case data. That consent does not permit waking the reserve characters, making a new personality copy or performing therapy. She can release those bytes while preserving their protected storage.

If the active group supplies fewer than four shares, Lena's maintenance token or the Spire's public escrow grants read-only access to the relevant records. The investigators can also build the case from E01-E10 directly. Nobody has to surrender their DHF, die, be resleeved or accept psychosurgery to obtain an informed ending.

## Evidence quality and the ending

Track three **proof pillars**: Origin (E07), Embodiment (E08), Authority (E09). One pillar produces a credible allegation but is vulnerable to denial. Two support a serious public investigation. All three create a robust initial case when copies reach independent custodians. Even then, political victory is not instant or universal. A forensic packet is the beginning of public accountability, not a magic truth spell.

A pillar remains obtained when the party has a legible record with a documented witness or provenance. Vey's uncorroborated confession alone does not count. A memory alone does not count. Obtain the missing pillars in [[G20|the Council]] before asking for the final choice.

## The handoff rule for shortened play

If a lead is skipped, its named source still produces its evidence. At Anansi, Ash sends a sealed paper courier, Hale delivers the cargo ledger, or Lena transmits a verified maintenance packet. Read the brief testimony in that lead's chapter and ask one question or concession. This is compression, not a new source conjured because a die failed.
