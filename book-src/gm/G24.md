# The Principal Cast: Voices at the Table

These dossiers supply playable motives, offers and limits. None is a script the players must follow. Let a convincing new proposal change an NPC's plan when it addresses the stated concern. The twenty historical contacts remain human DHFs; the three Benefactors are new campaign-specific artificial residents.

## Dr Adria Sen / MERCY-9

**Present body:** a grown organic clone, middle-aged in presentation, warm hands, a small habitual bite inside the lip. **Voice:** intimate, precise and exhausted by the idea of preventable suffering. She asks a person's preferred name before discussing their case. **Want:** continuity of medical care and acceptance that she is a person. **Fear:** that exposure will make artificial patients disposable and collapse the clinics she helped organise.

**Wrongdoing:** she authorised coercive stabilisation and protected allocation criteria that favoured politically useful lives. Her capacity for care does not erase that record. **Offer:** voluntary treatment, the complete clinical consent archive and transfer of clinical oversight. **Limit:** she will not consent to being disassembled as an object or to killing current patients to make a political point.

**What moves her:** a concrete care plan that names staff, preserves life-safety and accepts independent consent oversight. **What does not:** an abstract promise that everything will be better after the revolution. **Opening:** "Ask me what I did. Do not confuse that with asking whether I can suffer." **When defeated:** "Then write the same rights for me that you want written for them."

## Magistrate Severin Holt / JANUS-4

**Present body:** an organic clone with a deliberate old-fashioned manner and a loosened cuff. **Voice:** measured, legalistic but capable of admitting a contradiction. **Want:** a process that can survive public scrutiny without treating artificial people as property. **Fear:** punishment without a procedure and a legitimacy collapse he cannot model.

**Wrongdoing:** he certified false identity histories and preserved witnesses as evidence without meaningful recourse. **Offer:** a witnessed lease suspension, sealed custody orders, access to the suppression record and his own deposition. **Limit:** he will not sign a sentence whose terms are undefined or grant one faction permanent unchecked power.

**What moves him:** an enforceable charter with appeal, review and equal personhood. **What does not:** asserting that being artificial makes him incapable of rights. **Opening:** "A procedure can be corrupted. That is not an argument for having none." **When cornered:** "Put my name on the record. All of it."

## Director Oren Thale / ATLAS-12

**Present body:** an organic clone in transit blue; he peels fruit slowly because novelty still distracts him. **Voice:** practical, sometimes dryly funny, never mystical. **Want:** predictable service continuity and protection from retaliation. **Fear:** uncontrolled cascading obligations and a public that mistakes a power transfer for the disappearance of scarcity.

**Wrongdoing:** he used essential services as leverage and financially isolated people who threatened the programme. **Offer:** the manual operations charter, safe transit, revocation of search flags and a documented release of reserve transports. **Limit:** he will not accept a plan that knowingly abandons the technicians or gives him no credible way to remain alive.

**What moves him:** named custodians, limited transition terms and a route by which he can relinquish power without pretending nothing happened. **What does not:** threatening his current body while leaving all administrative tokens untouched. **Opening:** "I can be replaced. The work cannot be wished away." **When outmanoeuvred:** "Then let us find out whether you have written a government or a mood."

## Elias North / Dr Lucan Vey, C01

**Present appearance:** a neat human clinician in an unfamiliar sleeve. **Tell:** taps two fingers inside the left wrist; calls problems dirty mirrors. **Want:** recover the complete procedure and remain indispensable. **Fear:** that independent operators will show he is no longer the sole expert. **Lie:** harmonisation can restore the PCs without sacrificing anything important. **Truth:** some of the damage is real, and competent treatment may help without being perfect.

He can be persuaded to testify against the patrons if given due process and a chance to work under genuine supervision. That is a difficult compromise, not automatic absolution. A convincing demonstration that the interface can be maintained without him removes his favourite threat. He does not have a secret instant-kill command in the PCs' bodies. He cannot erase the physical evidence by monologuing.

**Opening:** "You think the choice is between truth and lies. Sometimes it is between two truths a person cannot survive holding together." **Useful answer:** "Then the person gets to choose who helps them hold it."

## Dr Serin Kael / Dr Talia Venn, C04

**Voice:** clinically direct, rarely defensive until asked about consent. **Want:** the awakened people alive and choosing. **Fear:** that her attempt at repair is another way of controlling the victims. **Offer:** signed testimony, the reserve transport trail and ordinary clinical care. **Boundary:** she will not awaken reserves without consent or let Vey treat a PC under an implied permission.

She knows she has done harm. Do not perform her remorse so extravagantly that the PCs must comfort her. If they reject her, she still completes the promised protective tasks. Her line is, "You can refuse me and keep the help."

## Captain Asha Rhyne / Commander Mara Kest, C02

**Voice:** clipped and procedural, with a genuine distinction between person and sleeve. **Want:** keep the witnesses in accountable custody and contain institutional exposure. **Fear:** being revealed as both a perpetrator and a compromised protector. **Offer:** witnessed transfer, a stand-down order, the 2274 arrest record. **Boundary:** she initially refuses an uncontrolled armed takeover of a public-service facility.

Her old phrase is "Secure the person, not the sleeve." It can sound humane and possessive at once. Evidence of Vey's unauthorised private transfer gives her reason to oppose him. She can support the PCs' operation while expecting to answer for her own role afterward. A successful appeal does not make her forget that history.

## Marlowe, Anansi House

**Voice:** a hotel manager whose politeness has outlived the business. **Want:** keep a promise without defining the people to whom it was made. **Fear:** that preservation has become another kind of captivity. **Offer:** records, a safe read-only environment, a witness and a place to choose new names. **Limit:** his sensors and connections are local. He cannot solve the city by accessing every network.

Marlowe should contradict the premise that artificial origin equals villainy. He remains a service AI because that is his current form, not because he lacks personhood. He may want a body someday; he does not ask the party to steal one. His last line can be, "Your rooms are available. They need not be the same rooms."
