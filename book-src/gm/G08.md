# SC01: The Resurrection Ward

**20 / 25 / 30 minutes.** Purpose: establish the new bodies, the missing century, the group bond and the first actionable threat. Cast: Dr Serin Kael. Evidence: E01 and the first half of E04. Fray: scheduled pulse 1. Exit: the recovery team arrives and the group chooses how to meet it.

## What is physically here

Six occupied beds stand in an old resleeving ward, with two clean, unoccupied bays behind a locked internal partition. All eight bed monitors show continuity serials matching the pregen IDs. The empty bays say **TRANSFERRED: OFF-SITE COLD ANNEX; NOT ACTIVATED**. A wall clock gives the current year as 2384. A paper medication trolley, a battered portable printer, six bags of recovered possessions and a hand-operated service door complete the room.

The PCs' packaged equipment is in those labelled bags. Let each character retrieve it without a roll. Do not begin by taking away equipment the sheet says they have. Serin can explain a sleeve's known needs in private; she will not examine anyone without permission. The windows show a strip of rainlit industrial canal, not an impossible view from the Aerium.

## Begin at the bedside

Read the opening in [[G00|Start Here]], then ask each player: "What name do you give her, and what do you check first?" Give a concrete answer about that character's current sleeve. A reflected unfamiliar face, a sensory delay or a missing habitual ache works better than announcing a catalogue of conditions.

Serin offers water to organic sleeves and a diagnostic lead to synthetics. "Neither is a test," she says. "You can refuse." Then she places six printed sheets on the trolley.

> "Your last registered year was 2274. This is 2384. You have not been awake for the interval. You were held as evidence. I have broken several laws to stop them moving you somewhere no law can reach."
>
> She puts both hands where you can see them. "That does not make me entitled to your trust."

Give [[H01|H01: Withdrawal Receipt]]. The year, custody classification and eight serials are automatic discoveries. The two reserve characters are intact. Serin will provide their transit reference immediately.

## Serin's answers

**Who are you?** "Dr Serin Kael. You knew me as Talia Venn. I can show you the paperwork. I will not ask you to believe a face." She checks pupils with her old three-count rhythm. This gives familiarity for characters linked to Talia, not full verification.

**Why these bodies?** "They were available, traceable enough to work, and cheap enough to buy quietly. I would rather be apologising for the fit than telling you I waited for perfection." Their former occupants' identities are not secretly trapped inside them. Known clinical needs have treatment plans; the next mystery is not involuntary possession.

**What did we do?** "You investigated Palimpsest. You found enough that the people running it could not release you and would not destroy you." She knows more than this about the interface, but is protecting the immediate escape. On direct questioning she admits she helped the programme before she helped its victims. That confession should occur now if earned; do not require the Anansi scene to play word for word later.

**Who paid?** "I sold access rights, called in debts and accepted help from people I do not admire. The accounting is waiting at Anansi House." This is true. She does not know the entire modern governing arrangement.

**Can I go back into storage?** "Yes. You are not volunteering for an investigation by waking up." She can arrange protected transfer alongside the reserves. Let the player choose a reserve pregen or another agreed role; do not punish the character for refusing the premise.

## First continuity pulse

After everyone has described one sensation, offer [[G04|pulse 1]]. Use a recognisable personal contrast: hands remembering the weight of a document stamp, a combat stance this body does not need, an old partner's name arriving before the present one. Ask, "Which part feels like yours?" The Fray rule decides risk; the player decides expression.

## Optional checks with explicit rewards

**Investigation D0:** read behind the withdrawal receipt's euphemisms. Success finds a scheduled private transfer after tonight's seal expiry; failure still establishes that they were held as evidence. No Heat cost: the paper is already here.

**Medicine or Data Analysis D0:** review the activation records. Success confirms there is no active remote-control receiver in the current sleeves and identifies a suppressed tracking receipt. Failure finds the tracking receipt but cannot establish when it left the clinic. The recovery team is coming either way; do not double-charge Heat for the scripted arrival.

**Read Person D0:** study Serin's behaviour. Success reveals that her fear intensifies at the word "consent," not at the idea of punishment. Failure means her composure gives no extra insight. It does not prove she is lying.

## The interruption

At the end of the scene, the portable printer wakes. It prints **CONTINUITY RECALL: SIX ACTIVE UNITS**. Serin tears the sheet away but does not conceal it. Footsteps arrive above the ward. A voice asks the front desk to confirm the number of occupied beds.

Serin points out the options without choosing for the party: answer the team through the intercom; leave through the hand-operated service door; set terms for surrender; prepare to resist. Move to [[G09|Breach of Cold Storage]]. If players leave earlier, stage the approach in the service corridor instead. Their decisiveness earns better positioning, not cancellation of the clue.
