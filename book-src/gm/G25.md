# The Twenty Contacts: A Playable Ensemble

Use three to five contacts prominently, plus brief personal appearances. The entire archive is available for depth, not a requirement to perform twenty introductions. Before play, note each selected PC's 2274 archive ally and loved one. Two meaningful links per active PC across the evening are enough. Current names and historical identities here are GM-only until identified and verified.

## Contact cards

**C01 - Elias North / Dr Lucan Vey.** The human architect; see the principal cast. Verify through independent old and current clinical signatures plus the witnessed succession ledger, not merely his wrist tap. His leverage is treatment; the party's leverage is proof he can be replaced.

**C02 - Captain Asha Rhyne / Commander Mara Kest.** Tactical commander; see the principal cast. The archived command certificate and current biometric signing record support her continuity claim. She squares objects before interrogation. Ask Imani what accountability would look like if custody can also save lives.

**C03 - Morrow Locke / Gideon Saye.** A human Meth financier who never drinks from the first glass offered. He wants the investment chain hidden. He offers untraceable funds, good sleeves and protected travel for a limited release. He will not willingly accept personal liability. Verify through an independently held escrow contract that spans his names. "There is a difference between a price and a bribe. We should discuss the difference before you refuse both." Use for Sabine or Latch, or as a remote offer when the party needs an escape stake.

**C04 - Dr Serin Kael / Dr Talia Venn.** Benefactor and compromised clinician; see the principal cast. Her three-count pupil check is familiarity; the signed medical licensing succession and Ash's preserved witness statement support verification. A PC can reject intimacy while accepting care.

**C05 - Mira Sol / Ysabel Chen.** Mutual-aid organiser who folds paper cranes while deciding what she can afford to promise. She wants forced resleeving ended and her neighbours protected. She offers reserve transport, witnesses and practical handover volunteers. She will not trade people's access to care for private romantic loyalty. A dated mutual-aid succession record and independent witness verify her claim. "You can help me. You cannot buy back the time." Especially useful for Sabine, and as a bridge between elite theory and street consequences.

**C06 - Brother Cinder / Osei Quade.** Chainbreaker organiser; thumb to brow before a promise, never calls a stored person cargo. He wants liberation but is tempted by the editing engine's ability to remove conditioning. He offers an exit cordon and volunteer witnesses. He refuses to serve a new permanent unelected master. Verify through an old prisoner-rescue deposition and current network custody records. "Show me how consent survives the emergency." Useful for Imani or any PC proposing to seize Palimpsest.

**C07 - Niko Saint / Dax Marrow.** Blue Lotus leader; coin across knuckles, debts outlive bodies. He offers ledgers and routes in exchange for protection or leverage. He wants innocent crew names redacted and would prefer his own decisions hidden too. Separate those demands. Verify through a preserved smuggling contract, not a coin trick. "I am not asking you to call me good. I am asking whether you need the door open." Use the Blue Lotus scene rather than adding another meeting.

**C08 - Father Ash / Hyeon Park.** Quiet Archive custodian; dates before names. He wants testimony protected and consent respected. He offers original paper, deposition and independent custody. He refuses compulsory resurrection. Verification comes through his witnessed record chain. "The dead are not witnesses on demand." His refusal never withholds the necessary paper clue.

**C09 - June Halden / Rhea Damar.** Continuity prosecutor who straightens her cuffs and says "for the record" in private. She wants a case that survives court while concealing her signature on the suppression. She offers a hearing and lawful evidence custody. A PC can insist her old role be included; she may accept in exchange for due process. Verify using two court registry entries and a retained witness signature. "A true accusation can still be mishandled." Use for Solenne, especially when deciding whether publication and admissibility can coexist.

**C10 - Jalen Cross / Petar Volkov.** Bounty hunter; checks exits first, rolls one shoulder. He wants payment and refuses contracts explicitly demanding stack destruction. He offers a temporary stand-down or a witnessed transfer. Verification comes through an old contract bond and current broker escrow, not body language alone. "Alive is not the same as unharmed. I know that. I am trying to keep it in the contract." He can return in the finale as an exit escort if treated as a negotiable person.

**C11 - Lena Orr / Keiko Marr.** Engineer; counts handshakes backwards and knows Latch's obsolete error nickname. She wants the logs preserved without becoming the sole scapegoat. She offers the maintenance token and civilian-service expertise. She will not promise to resume a former relationship tonight. Verify through independent employment succession and an Ash-held personal deposition. "Keep the evidence. Keep a place for the person who brought it."

**C12 - Soren Gray / Arun Bell.** Rights architect; draws nested circles, asks who owns the copy. He wants control to belong to persons rather than infrastructure owners. He offers an audit protocol and a rights-respecting temporary charter. He refuses unrestricted copying of living DHFs as a convenience. Verification comes from a published technical design deposited before his current sleeve and an independent succession record. "Access is not consent." Useful for Anik or any group drafting a new government.

**C13 - Vivian Locke / Celeste Ardan.** Human Meth dynast; no contractions when angry, recreated signet scar. She wants the fortune insulated from the programme. She offers extraordinary restitution funds if the party narrows individual culpability accurately. She refuses a blanket admission to crimes not supported by evidence. Verify through a physical trust seal and external executor's record. "Name what I paid for. Then name what I knew." Her defence may be partly valid; the funding trail still matters. Use as a call, not another mandatory scene.

**C14 - Tom Arlen / Emil Navarro.** Detective; pauses before a suspect's name, says "blue rain" for a compromised scene. He wants an intact chain of evidence and Nia alive without pretending the lost century did not happen. He offers introductions, lawful witness protection and an honest relationship conversation. He will not erase his current commitments. Independent service records and Ash's paper deposition support verification. "I did not stop loving the person. I stopped organising my life around their absence."

**C15 - Rin Azad / Farah Nouri.** Psychosurgeon; corrects terminology even during an argument, touches the bridge of her nose at a neural map. She wants the original procedure and fears her own memories were edited. She offers a second clinical opinion independent of Vey. She refuses to accept Edda's authority merely because they share a past. Verify through a medical succession ledger and a retained patient-consent witness. "Tell me what you can prove before you tell me what I remember." Useful for Imani's intimate bond and Edda's professional rivalry; never require those links to become a jealousy plot.

**C16 - Hale Mercer / Juno Sera.** Broker; four-note hum, never sits with their back to glass. They want to survive, profit and keep Cass alive. Their relationship with Anik can also be important. They offer a ledger, a second vehicle and restitution for selling the awakening confirmation. They will not pretend a confession erases the sale. Verify through broker succession and an independent Anansi guest-record chain. "You are allowed to leave after I tell the truth." Do not force a romantic triangle between PCs.

**C17 - Ren Ko / Hanzo Irei.** Extraction specialist; snaps gloves, addresses people by contract role. He wants the route fragment suppressed and his employers insulated. He offers a buyout or a private departure if evidence remains sealed. He retreats when his suppression request is publicly recorded. Verify through an old extraction retainer and a separate current licence. "You have evidence. I have terms." Use him as pressure at Blue Lotus or replace one security lead; do not add another fight just to use the card.

**C18 - Lark Seven / Delia Frost.** Virtuality sculptor; one deliberately impossible shadow in every room. They want their work credited and control of the architecture returned. They offer a clean read-only visualisation or a second technical interpretation. They will not endorse erasing consent barriers for elegant design. Verify through old deposited source signatures and current professional escrow. "Real enough for whom?" Useful if Anik wants a professional reckoning rather than a romantic one. Their credit grievance is genuine but not proof Anik knowingly stole.

**C19 - Deacon Marr / Amos Dain.** Theologian; prayer beads with one missing bead, a short blessing before touching a stack. He wants to distinguish guilt from implanted guilt and preserve voluntary belief. He offers a written deposition and a grounding conversation for Edda. He refuses to equate suffering with deserved punishment. Verify through old paper teaching notes, a witnessed succession statement and the beads' custody record as corroboration. "A memory of guilt is not a verdict. Responsibility still asks what you do next."

**C20 - Iris Tan / Sera Bale.** Defence advocate; handwritten notes first, "consent survives the sleeve." She wants the witnesses legally free and the sealed order exposed. She offers audit admission, compact drafting and protected evidence custody. She refuses to sign away another person's autonomy without authority. Verify through independently held client consents and bar succession records. "I can make the promise enforceable. I cannot make it honest. We should arrange for both." Essential substitute when the party needs a lawful path, personally important to Sabine, Cass or Solenne.

## Substitution without retconning history

When a spotlighted pregen is not selected, the same evidence is still present. Let another PC speak to the contact, or use their own recorded ally for the conversation while retaining the actual source of the record. Do not rewrite C11's historical identity to fit a different romance. The forty archived sleeves and 160 links remain the source of who knew whom.

A contact can occupy several roles across different lifetimes. That is deliberate. Ask which historical period a player refers to before adjudicating a contradiction. Being an enemy in one sleeve and an ally later is not automatically evidence of an impostor.
