# Lead C: Relay Seventeen

**Approximately 20 minutes.** Main contact: Lena Orr / Keiko Marr (C11). Optional support: Soren Gray / Arun Bell (C12). Evidence: E03, E06 and the maintenance knowledge needed for civilian protection.

## The scene

> Relay Seventeen rises out of the canal like a tuning fork. Inside, fans move warm air over cabinets older than the building around them. A woman kneeling beside an open panel counts a handshake backwards under her breath.
>
> "Three, two, one. There you are. You always did arrive after the error message."

Lena is talking to a machine. If Latch responds with the old diagnostic nickname, she freezes and looks up. Her surprise is genuine. Soren, if present, asks the practical question: "Who owns the copy we are about to make?"

## Lena's problem

The company plans to clear Relay Seventeen's cold diagnostic store at the renewal. Lena has copied a catalogue, but the oldest records are signed to an obsolete audit credential. She can extract them now at the cost of revealing that she used an employee key. She wants the PCs to guarantee that she will not be left as the only named source when the story breaks.

A simple promise to copy her evidence to a second custodian and take her with them is sufficient. A PC need not give a speech or pass Diplomacy to understand professional solidarity. If the group refuses to help her personally, she still gives the unclassified migration log; she will not volunteer her credential unless offered another safeguard.

## The information

Give [[H05|H05]]. It establishes that the Foundation inherited Palimpsest's storage domains and that three client certificates persist through repeated changes in public names. Their technical roots predate their asserted current biographies. That suggests fabricated succession, but could still fit a human dynastic fraud. A genesis register is needed to establish artificial origin.

Lena can also supply a sealed maintenance token, [[H09|H09]], which carries read-only audit rights. It cannot overwrite people or grant unlimited city control. In the finale, it opens the service admission path and can delay Renewal for one operation round. Using it for that delay marks it spent for further delays, but does not invalidate copied evidence.

## The task

The terminal offers **READ / MIRROR / PURGE**. Its purge function is the scheduled corporate process, not a trap disguised as a normal button. Read gives the essential log without a roll. Mirror creates a forensic copy with a useful proof of custody. Data Engineering or Digital Networking D0 completes Mirror without a trace. Failure still produces the copy but burns Lena's employee credential and raises Heat by one. She must then leave with the party or relocate through Mira.

Purge destroys the local cache after the copy is made, concealing exactly what was accessed but depriving later investigators of the original. Explain that tradeoff. It is a deliberate evidence decision, not an automatic consequence of a failed roll. A paper printout alone records content; Lena's witnessed signature and device log preserve its provenance.

## The personal conversation

For Latch, Lena offers a single honest sentence before any attempt to resume intimacy: "I learned to stop waiting for your handshake. I do not know how to start again in one night." She can affirm their historical bond without claiming the relationship is unchanged.

For Anik, Soren says, "You taught me that a copy is not a promise. I have spent a century arguing the promise belongs to the person, not the owner of the machine." He wants Anik's position on whether the archive should ever hold editable copies of living witnesses.

If neither relevant PC is selected, give the scene to the investigator who has most often treated an NPC as a resource. Lena's question is useful to anyone: "Am I coming with you, or am I your source until the first subpoena?"

## The practical revelation

A schematic on the wall shows three separate buses: **LIFE-SAFETY**, **CIVIC AUTHORITY**, and **PALIMPSEST CLINICAL**. Lena explains that they share a facility but are not the same circuit. Root administrators made them appear inseparable to discourage oversight. Independent staff can continue emergency services under a limited manual charter if the authority lease is revoked.

Give a preliminary explanation of E10, but reserve the complete control diagram for the core. This is how you prevent a false finale choice between tolerating tyranny and indiscriminately killing civilians. The party still needs to perform the safe handover; it is not automatic just because they know it exists.

## Interruption and exit

At Heat 3 or higher, a supervisor calls and asks why the old diagnostic store is active. A PC can answer as a lawful auditor, let Lena claim a fault, or disconnect and leave. Bureaucracy or Expression D0 avoids adding a personal employment charge to Lena's case. A failure does not create another Heat increase if Mirror already did so; use the existing consequence.

Lena gives Anansi House her arrival estimate over a hardline. If this lead is skipped, her signed packet reaches Marlowe before the PCs, and she joins by audio to ask the same protection question. The maintenance token remains available; she does not withhold essential access because the table selected another branch.
