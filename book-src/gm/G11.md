# Lead A: The Quiet Archive

**Approximately 20 minutes, compressed within the street allocation.** Main contact: Father Ash / Hyeon Park (C08). Optional personal contact: Tom Arlen for Nia, Deacon Marr for Edda, Iris Tan for Solenne or Sabine. Evidence: E01, E03 and the ethical limits of the reserve-share release.

## The scene

> The chapel has no network displays. Names are written on strips of paper and pinned above shallow bowls of water. A man in a patched clerical coat finishes reading a date before he looks at you.
>
> "I have been keeping your absence in order," he says. "I would prefer not to begin again."

Ash offers seating and a dry envelope. He will not touch a stack without consent. He is willing to help before the PCs promise anything. His request is that they let the archive retain a witnessed copy of any custody evidence they acquire and protect the right of stored people not to be awakened merely to solve a case.

## What Ash places on the table

The envelope contains an original storage order and the trust deed transferring Palimpsest's remaining assets to the Continuity Foundation. Give [[H03|H03]]. The paper's age, continuous custody log and witness signatures are available for inspection. The deposition is not immune to ordinary forgery, but its provenance is independent of the PC memories and the Foundation's live network.

Ash also has a photograph of the PCs' former group, taken before the raid, with their names recorded by separate witnesses. That helps verify historical associations. It does not prove that a current face is occupied by the same mind. For Ash himself, a successive chain of signed consent statements and his documented custody of the archive establish the strongest available continuity claim.

## The roleplay dilemma

A sealed stack in the cabinet belonged to a deceased clerk who witnessed a transfer. Ash refuses to spin it up because the clerk expressly declined resurrection. June Halden has argued that public interest should override that refusal. The witness's **already recorded paper deposition** is on file and sufficient for tonight's clue.

Say this before players decide: "Ash tells you the deposition is usable. Waking the clerk is not required to get the evidence." The choice concerns whether the party supports a boundary when violating it would be convenient. A PC who accepts the boundary earns Ash's personal endorsement and the Quiet Archive as an independent publication custodian. A PC who argues for compulsory testimony still receives the deed, but Ash asks Iris to supervise later handling.

## Questions and answers

**Why preserve us for 110 years?** "The order calls you evidence that must remain available. The private renewal clause calls you a liability that must never become public." He can point to both passages.

**Did we authorise a memory wipe?** "One signature appears on one clinical exception. Eight names appear on the subsequent certification. Those are not the same document." He has the certification, not the original exception sheet; the Theatre node or Serin supplies the other half.

**Do you know who controls the Foundation?** "I know the names on the trusts. I do not know the people inside those names." The public trustees are Sen, Holt and Thale through intermediaries. This is a lead, not a declaration of AI identity.

**Are you the person I remember?** "I can offer you my chain of custody. I cannot give back the years in between." If a relationship player asks what changed, Ash admits the archive is less absolute than the religion he once preached: he now helps people who choose resleeving while maintaining consent for those who do not.

## Checks and complications

**Investigation or Bureaucracy D0:** a success spots a custodial-renewal clause that will let Iris challenge the recall order. That advantage can cancel one later legal obstacle. On a miss, the clue packet remains complete; no extra cost applies.

**Diplomacy D1:** ask Ash to leave with the group as a live witness. Success means he entrusts the chapel to another custodian and accompanies them. Failure means he stays, but offers a recorded, witnessed deposition instead. Threatening the archive never produces better evidence.

At Heat 4 or higher, an official arrives asking to inspect the custody log. Let the party hold a lawful conversation, redirect the inspection or leave through the public back door. The official does not burn the chapel. An aggressive response may expose the group's route; warn before applying Heat.

## A personal line

For Nia, Tom says, "I had an entire life after you. That is not an accusation." For Edda, Deacon Marr says, "I kept the bead you said I would lose. I lost the certainty instead." For Solenne, Ash asks, "Will you publish the names of victims, or only the names of those who used them?" Use one, not all three.

## Compression handoff

If skipped, Ash sends the sealed deed to Anansi with a courier whose chain-of-custody signature is recorded. His attached note asks the same consent question. The party gets a short call with him before final publication. The ethical choice is preserved without adding a full visit.
