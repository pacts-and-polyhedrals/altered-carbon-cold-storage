# SC02: Breach of Cold Storage

**15 / 20 / 25 minutes.** Purpose: teach action resolution through a custody dispute, not a mandatory fight. Cast: Jalen Cross and two recovery personnel; Captain Asha Rhyne on a secure call. Evidence: E02. Fray: none scheduled. Exit: the Rainline, or escorted admission to the Spire if the party knowingly surrenders.

## The opening position

The retrieval party has three functional zones: the ward entrance, the corridor with a reinforced viewing panel, and the service stair. Each move between adjacent zones is possible under the supplied action rules. The ward's partition offers cover; the medicine trolley is movable cover; the service door has a mechanical latch. There are no secretly locked escape routes.

Jalen Cross stops behind the viewing panel. He checks the exits, rolls his right shoulder and speaks to the camera rather than pointing a gun at a patient.

> "My contract is for intact stacks. You are worth more with people inside you. We can all make this unpleasant, or we can read the small print."
>
> A second voice enters his earpiece, loud enough to hear: "Secure the person, not the sleeve."

That second voice is Asha. Imani or anyone with the corresponding historical relationship can recognise the phrase as familiarity. Do not grant automatic confirmation of current identity.

## What the team wants

Jalen wants payment without participating in Real Death. His two personnel want a routine transfer. Asha wants the investigators in official custody rather than Vey's private custody. The order contains a private corporate countersignature that conflicts with its apparent public purpose. None of these people initially knows the AI revelation.

Give [[H02|the recall order]] as soon as someone asks to see authority, intercepts the nearby printout, searches a dropped case or records the team's spoken warrant reference. The handout cannot depend on winning the encounter.

## Four complete approaches

**Read the contract.** A player points out the private destination and asks who takes responsibility for disputed custody. No roll is needed to make the discrepancy real. Bureaucracy or Diplomacy D0 persuades Jalen to hold for verification. Success buys a clean departure through the service stair while he calls Asha; failure still buys departure, but he records their sleeve descriptions and Heat rises by one. Explain that risk before rolling. A personal appeal to his refusal to destroy stacks is an alternative: he will accept a witnessed promise to attend an independent hearing.

**Make a better offer.** Jalen will take a signed contact commitment from Iris Tan or a genuine negotiating stake, not imaginary money. A PC may offer an interview after the case is copied to independent custody. Expression D1 can sell an audacious bluff, but a clear, enforceable custody proposal needs only Diplomacy D0. On failure he demands one escorted representative. The party can refuse and change approach; a failed negotiation is not mind control.

**Slip out.** The service latch is stiff, not encrypted. Mechanics or Athletics D0 opens it quietly; automatic opening with noise is available. One useful Stealth contribution hides the group's direction. On a failed concealment, the team knows they entered the Rainline and Heat rises by one, but the stair is not magically blocked. Serin remains briefly to close the ward and then rejoins them at Anansi by a separate route.

**Resist.** Use Jalen as ADV02, supported by two ADV03 profiles, with nonlethal custody as their goal. They demand surrender after the first effective exchange. Play one complete intent/check/resolution cycle, then reassess morale. If a teammate is badly hurt, a stack is endangered, or the order's defect becomes public, Jalen withdraws. Use the objective and resources in [[G27|Threats]], not an endless damage race.

## If the players attack first

Let the consequence fit the action. A warning shot or disabling attack raises the tension, not necessarily Heat. Public lethal violence that threatens patients raises Heat by one and makes Asha more insistent on witnessed custody. Jalen still refuses to target stacks. Announce clearly if someone chooses an action that might destroy a stack. No adversary executes a helpless PC as a lesson.

## If they are overwhelmed

They are taken intact to the Spire's clinical reception under a disputed custody order. Play a short transport conversation with Asha. She gives them the recall order, asks who can verify their identities, and permits a call to Iris or Marlowe because an undocumented transfer would implicate her. Use [[G19|the custody route]]. Fold the street sources and Anansi testimony into reception visitors or supervised calls. Defeat changes leverage, not the availability of the adventure.

## The departing hook

A recovered order includes the line **"Archive-compatible subjects; do not perform unsupervised extraction."** Jalen admits he has never seen that condition before. On the street, Serin gives the party Anansi House's address and three source names: Ash for paper, Saint for cargo, Orr for relays.

> "Do not try to recognise the city," she says. "Find something in it that can still be checked."

Move to [[G10|The Rainline]].
