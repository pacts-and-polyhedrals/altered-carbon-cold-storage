# SC06, Part One: Entering the Spire

**About 15 / 20 / 25 minutes within SC06.** Purpose: turn accumulated relationships and evidence into access. Do not run all entry routes. Cast: Asha, Vey, Lena or the contact supporting the chosen method. Renewal has not started.

## The threshold

> Above the canal, the Spire's lower windows are filled with warm light. The public entrance advertises a clinic, an arbitration office and a transit-benefit centre. All three use the same reception desk.
>
> Far overhead, a ring of lights is counting down to a ceremony the city has not been invited to attend.

The party already knows the destination. They do not need to find another clue merely to enter. Ask them for an approach, a desired witness and one condition they refuse to surrender.

## Public audit

Iris, Tom, Ash or a successful Court petition can sponsor a lawful read-only inspection. Asha meets the group in reception. She will require weapons secured in an accessible monitored locker and forbid unsupervised connections to current DHFs. Those conditions apply to the Foundation's personnel too. The party can negotiate alternative safeguards.

Bureaucracy or Diplomacy D0 improves the terms: an independent witness enters, their recorder remains active, or their copies go to an outside custodian before the meeting. Failure means one of those advantages must be earned at the council; it does not cancel admission. At Heat six, Asha frames the meeting as supervised custody rather than a voluntary audit. The PCs are still intact and can speak.

## Maintenance access

Lena's token, Niko's canal code or Cass's recovered route reaches the service lift. Describe a locked grate, a pump walkway and a staffed maintenance station. No battle map is necessary. The crew wants to know whether the party will endanger life-safety systems. A truthful promise and showing the separation diagram earns cooperation.

Mechanics, Engineering or Digital Networking D0 bypasses the remaining administrative gate. Success reaches the core unnoticed long enough to copy the genesis register first. Failure triggers a polite lock-and-call procedure: Vey invites them upstairs, accompanied by security. Heat rises by one if appropriate, but the route still reaches the meeting. Never treat a maintenance worker as an automatic hostile minion.

## Negotiated invitation

Vey has sent an offer. The party can demand a read-only body assay, outside recording, safe custody of reserve stacks or treatment without surrendering personality. He accepts the first two because he believes the evidence will persuade them that his work is necessary. He can guarantee local noninterference with Serin's reserve transport, but cannot truthfully guarantee every outside investor's behaviour.

A well-supported challenge to his personal dependency on the patrons wins an additional concession without a roll if recovered in the Theatre. Otherwise Diplomacy D1 secures it; failure exposes the nature of his limit. He will not give the group administrator write access before the meeting simply because a charm check succeeds.

## Custody route

If taken during the breach, the PCs arrive in clinical reception rather than a torture chamber. Asha records each stack's intact status and the limits of authorised contact. Iris can intervene by call; Marlowe's read-only archive can be accessed in a supervised interview room; street witnesses send their packets. Run the relevant evidence scenes here in compressed form, then admit the group to the core.

Custody carries a real cost: Asha controls their immediate exit and they have not earned a clean maintenance entrance. It also supplies a useful witness to the Foundation's conduct. Do not strip players of every bargaining instrument just because they lost a fight.

## Assault

A frontal attack is possible, but tell the players what they can see: staffed public services below, one fortified lift, and security that can retreat upwards. Use a single objective encounter against two ADV03 personnel and one ADV04 lead for six PCs. Their aim is to block the lift and preserve stacks, not fight to the last person.

A three-contribution breach works as a cinematic alternative to tactical combat: suppress or distract the guards, open the lift, and protect bystanders. Each clear, plausible contribution uses one appropriate D0/D1 check with a stated cost. Failed opening attempts lead to Vey opening the lift under negotiation, not an impenetrable door. Actual combat damage and action timing use the supplied system; do not run both procedures for the same action.

Public lethal violence raises Heat and weakens a clean public narrative. It does not make the underlying evidence false. If the PCs take this path, give them a chance to establish witnesses and civilian safeguards before the final decision.

## The final corridor

The chosen route ends at three labelled doors: **LIFE-SAFETY OPERATIONS**, **CIVIC ROOT**, and **CLINICAL PALIMPSEST**. Through the first, ordinary technicians are arguing about a pump fault. Through the second, the three Benefactors wait. Through the third, organic clones sleep in diagnostic cradles.

A paper procedure is fixed beside the life-safety door. Any player who examines it learns that local operators can maintain essential services if the civic authority lease is suspended. Give the complete control diagram, [[H13|H13]], now or immediately after the reveal. No knowledge roll is necessary to avoid the false "kill the city" dilemma.
