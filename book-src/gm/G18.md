# Memory Node C: The Last Broadcast

**Within SC05: about 10-15 minutes.** Spotlight: Latch, Cass and Solenne; every active PC receives one sentence. Fray: scheduled pulse 4. Handout: H09; personal prompts in G26.

## The record

> Rain moves across a room you almost remember. Eight people stand around a transmitter. Their bodies are the bodies you lost. Nobody is delivering a heroic speech. Someone is trying to stop a hand from shaking. Someone is checking the door.
>
> A voice says, "Four shares can tell the story. No single person should have to carry all of it."
>
> Another answers, "And the people who wake up can decide what to do. We are not leaving them an order."

That final line is the group's documented instruction. It prevents the previous selves from commandeering present agency.

## What the earlier party had assembled

The archive contains names of compromised officials, an encryption seed, funding and ownership records, raid security recordings, transfer routes, the editing procedure, a virtual architecture path and a publication key. These match the eight pregen fragments exactly. Show the diagram as a redundant case archive, not eight coloured locks. Any four stored shares reconstruct its readable content; independent evidence remains available even if none are surrendered.

The publication key signs the packet's provenance. It does not force a broadcast, grant control over minds or make every recipient believe it. The audit credential grants a read-only inspection of the Spire's root interface. It can be used with Lena's token or the public escrow copy. The physical separation between life-safety, civic authority and clinical editing is visible in the old diagram and still exists.

Give [[H09|H09: Audit Route]].

## The unresolved moment

The final camera view shows the team approaching a door marked **CLIENT EMBODIMENT ATTESTATION**. Before they can open it, Asha's second team breaches the service entrance. The recording ends. This is why the former sleeves nearly discovered the ultimate truth but did not complete verification. Do not play an unseen earlier revelation and then arbitrarily wipe it again.

The current party is now in a position their old selves never reached. They can inspect the current bodies, compare origins and document actual governing authority in the same place during renewal.

## Six voices, briefly

Ask each selected PC one question from [[G26|the spotlight pages]], or simply: "What would you tell that earlier version of yourself?" Limit each first response to a sentence. Allow a second response only if it creates an exchange between PCs rather than six monologues. A player can decline and let a recorded hand gesture stand for the moment.

Now offer pulse 4. The conflict is ownership of past intention. A current PC may disagree with their previous self without being wrong. An accepted echo can reveal a mundane detail that strengthens a relationship: who carried the other's coat, who checked medication, who refused to leave the last person behind.

## Optional actions

**Digital Networking or Data Engineering D0:** validate the credential. Success lets the party choose a clean service entrance or public audit appointment at the Spire, cancelling one entry obstacle. Failure still validates it, but a trace appears and the Spire expects them; apply Heat +1 only if this scene has not already incurred incidental Heat.

**Expression or Investigation D0:** reconstruct the intended public accusation from its draft. Success produces a clear victim-redacted summary, useful for publication. Failure leaves the complete evidence but requires editing later. The group does not accidentally broadcast because the roll failed.

**Stealth or Navigation D0**, framed as analysing Cass's route rather than physically moving in the memory: success recovers a canal access phrase usable at the maintenance lift. Failure leaves the map; a modern operator or code is still needed.

## Voluntary overreach

A PC can attempt to recover the last unfinished message to the future. Warn of the standard +2 Fray and raw 1d6 Ego loss. The reward is a signed emergency instruction from the original maintenance team: the civic authority lease can be suspended while life-safety services pass to a distributed human-and-AI operator charter. This counts as one completed **control** contribution during the final operation, but never as the civilian-safety handover itself.

The message says, "Do not confuse removing a ruler with switching off a city." It is a safeguard, not a mandate to choose revocation.

## The invitation

As the archive closes, Elias North sends a message through the audit channel:

> "You have reached the same door again. This time, I am prepared to open it. Come as witnesses, not cargo. There are things you deserve to see before you decide what to destroy."

The offer is sincere in its limited way: Vey wants voluntary cooperation and believes the patrons can justify themselves. He is not surrendering authority. The party can accept, use another route or send conditions in advance. Proceed to [[G19|Spire Entry]].
