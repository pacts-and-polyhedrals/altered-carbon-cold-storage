# Eight Investigators, Forty Echoes

These prompts extend, rather than replace, the existing sleeve histories. They are optional sensory invitations. A player may reshape the emotion while retaining a verified external event. Never require every listed echo to appear. Choose one early memory and one late reckoning per selected PC.

## PC01 - Nia Calder, Continuity Investigator

**Fragment:** names of compromised officials. **Current complication:** disaster-response reflexes notice a threat before Nia has decided whether it is one. **2274 archive links:** enemy Vey; rival Mara Kest; ally Hyeon Park; loved one Emil Navarro.

**Archive echoes:** Jessa Calder remembers the satisfying pressure of a records stamp and a death certificate that should not have existed. Kwame Soro remembers a border official calling identity laundering an administrative correction. Mei Arata remembers salt on a customs rail while a manifest hides stolen Elder material. Alexei Rourke remembers a Blue Lotus contact offering the right answer for the wrong reason. Nia's last sleeve remembers arranging eight names so no witness could be quietly omitted.

**Early cue:** the ward receipt calls living people units. **Personal scene:** Tom can verify the historical bond while explaining his present commitments. **Late question:** "If the law preserves the record but refuses the person, which do you protect first?" **Overreach reward:** a specific countersignature connecting the private transfer to Vey's office; it gives one legal concession, not automatic victory.

**Fracture suggestion:** Nia stops treating institutional recognition as the final proof of self, or integrates an earlier insistence that no case is worth abandoning a named witness. The player defines the cost. **Private prompt card:** [[PC01-CARD|Nia's Echo]].

## Using the optional rewards

All rewards above require the same warned overreach cost if accessed through dangerous recall. Do not charge again when a reward was already obtained in its scene. The same reward can often be earned safely through a contact or normal evidence work. These options make personal risk useful, never compulsory. The finale's maximum of one pre-completed contribution per track still applies.


## Extra verified early-life preludes and disputed records

Sabine, Imani, Anik and Solenne have one or two additional verified prelude bodies to establish their seventh or eighth current sleeve. Those stories are found in the archived Sleeve Items; they are earlier than the five original recorded lives and do not replace the documented relationships.

For Nia and Cass, their earliest four verified historical bodies span 2274. The fifth original archived dossier is a **contested Palimpsest ledger claim**, not a confirmed additional body, and its four relationship leads remain usable as evidence. Play the personal memories as real possibilities and allow outside corroboration; do not pronounce every cherished relationship a lie. The final archive's disputed extra body should not be imposed as a sixth current sleeve.

No character can be excluded from the central case because a prelude has no four named relationships. The original 160 contact links, eight evidence shares and four-of-eight redundancy remain unchanged.
