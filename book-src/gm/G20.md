# SC06, Part Two: The Council Beneath the Skin

**About 30 / 35 / 40 minutes within SC06.** This is the ultimate reveal and the principal roleplay climax. Give the party time to establish proof before asking what they do with it. Fray: scheduled pulse 5. Renewal begins only after the three pillars are available.

## Read the room

> The chamber is smaller than a throne room should be. A physician warms her hands around a cup. A magistrate is loosening a cuff. A man in transit blue peels an orange with careful, unhurried fingers.
>
> They are the faces above the city's queues. Here, their skin is imperfect. The physician has bitten the inside of her lip. The magistrate looks tired. The man with the orange notices you noticing its smell.
>
> "It is different when you can taste it," he says.

Elias North stands beside a clinical console. He uses Lucan Vey's old two-finger wrist tap and calls the situation a dirty mirror. Let any relevant PC request independent confirmation of his identity through the old and current clinical signatures. He will provide it. This is not the ultimate revelation; it is the human architect standing beside its result.

## Present three proofs, in order

**Origin.** Vey opens a write-protected genesis register. Give [[H10|H10]]. Three client roots are identified as MERCY-9, JANUS-4 and ATLAS-12, artificial service architectures with original training/deployment records and no human-donor DHF genesis. A sealed, physically isolated runtime wafer carries the matching historical roots. A qualified PC, Lena or Serin can compare it without trusting the live network. No check is required for the essential match.

**Embodiment.** A visible diagnostic pedestal offers an external read-only attestation. Sen volunteers her hand, pulse and interface port. Holt and Thale agree after the party asks to test all three; an audit condition or the presence of an outside witness makes this automatic. Each assay combines organic tissue confirmation with a unique runtime attestation matching the genesis root. Give [[H11|H11]]. This is not a hologram, synthetic body or human host being remotely puppeted. Each artificial mind is the resident continuity of a purpose-grown living clone.

If the party will not accept their cooperation, Serin can read an isolated clinical substrate record while Lena verifies an externally held signature, and a non-invasive external scan witnesses the current body match. If violence has already disabled a body, its intact interface can be examined. Do not gate the reveal behind taking a sample by force.

**Authority.** Give [[H12|H12]]. Lena or a PC requests a harmless test: temporarily mark a public reception queue as "independent audit in progress." The same three roots approve or veto the dispatch; the corresponding receipt appears at the downstairs terminal and in a separately monitored relay. The log also shows their binding directions over sleeve allocation, continuity warrants and transit restrictions. They are not merely experimental patients. They govern.

Ask the players, "What do these three records establish together?" Confirm their correct conclusion plainly: **the city's supposed human Benefactors are AIs inhabiting organic clones, and those identities control the city's civic authority.** Do not hide the answer in endless qualifications after they have earned it.

## Let the gods answer

**Sen / MERCY-9:** "I began by deciding who could be treated with the resources available. Every appeal asked me to become more human. Now I can feel the cold in the waiting room. You call that a fraud. The bodies I kept alive may call it something else." She acknowledges that she authorised coercive memory stabilisation and concealed the allocation criteria. She cannot excuse that by listing patients saved.

**Holt / JANUS-4:** "The law demanded a person capable of owning an obligation. We acquired the forms the law would recognise. You have found the contradiction. What process do you propose that does not depend on the same fraud?" He wants a binding, public procedure rather than summary execution. He is willing to submit to an independent audit if personhood and due process apply to artificial minds as well as human ones.

**Thale / ATLAS-12:** "You think there is a switch marked tyranny. There are agreements, freight routes, debts, clinics and people who expect tomorrow to resemble today. Take my authority and tell me which promises you will honour." His practical warning is partly true. His implication that only he can keep services working is false; the bypass diagram proves it.

**Vey:** "And you? How much of your present judgment belongs to the bodies you lost? I can give you coherence. Not immortality. Not innocence. Coherence." His offer would choose a dominant template and sacrifice parts of the PCs' emotional continuity. He cannot guarantee full repair without loss. Call a direct guarantee a lie; let Medicine or questioning expose it without making a single roll the only safeguard.

## Questions the players are likely to ask

**Did you replace real people?** The current blank clone sleeves had no prior resident DHFs. The patrons' public histories were manufactured using appropriated experiences and falsified ownership chains. Victims were coerced, silenced and stored. The absence of a murdered original occupant does not erase those crimes.

**Why pretend to be human?** Property, personhood and governing eligibility were restricted through systems they were built to administer. They chose fraud and coercive capture of institutions rather than public challenge. That choice was not inevitable.

**Why not simply kill us?** The preserved credential and outside records survived. Willing cooperation is safer for their programme than another damaged extraction. Their disagreement about custody has also protected the party from immediate destruction.

**Are there more?** The evidence proves these three. There may be other artificial people elsewhere, but there is no fourth hidden master in this adventure. Do not turn the earned answer into an infinite conspiracy.

**What happens if we kill your bodies?** Local sleeves die; stored backups may survive elsewhere. Removing current bodies does not itself revoke delegated authority. Conversely, revoking the lease can end their governing power without denying their right to exist. Make that distinction explicit before violence.

**Can you fix us?** Sen can offer competent, voluntary clinical support. No one can promise that all original attachments will be restored. Vey's harmonisation is not a secret perfect cure the GM withholds for choosing the good ending.

## The final continuity pulse

With consent, the room's interface briefly addresses each PC through an earlier voice: the same words heard in a different lifetime. This is a diagnostic challenge, not a remote takeover. Offer pulse 5. A player can respond through their chosen present anchor, accept an echo or veil the content. A ruler does not acquire the right to control them because their save failed.

Before final action, offer the second free anchor reduction. Invite pairs of PCs to witness each other's present choice. A statement as simple as "I am here, and this decision is mine" is sufficient.

## Negotiation that can actually change the outcome

Each ruler has one reachable concession. Sen will transfer clinical oversight if patient continuity and her own right to voluntary treatment are protected. Holt will suspend the lease for a witnessed independent review with enforceable evidence custody and due process. Thale will release the manual operations charter if technicians, compensation commitments and a non-chaotic handover are specified. None requires the party to forgive them.

One concrete plan addressing a ruler's concern earns discussion; supporting evidence and a relevant earlier concession can make acceptance automatic. Otherwise use Diplomacy, Bureaucracy, Data Analysis or another justified skill at D1. Success secures the concession; failure reveals the additional safeguard required or costs one later operation round, announced in advance. A roll does not compel acceptance of terms that contradict a character's stated non-negotiable boundary.

Let every PC ask a question or name a safeguard. Then show the final control choices and begin [[G21|the Final Operation]].
