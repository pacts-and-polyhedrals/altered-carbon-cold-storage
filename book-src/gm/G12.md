# Lead B: Blue Lotus, Black Water

**Approximately 20 minutes.** Main contact: Niko Saint / Dax Marrow (C07). Personal contact: Hale Mercer / Juno Sera (C16), especially for Cass or Anik. Evidence: E03, clone-delivery routes and a canal approach to the Spire.

## The scene

> The old bathhouse still smells faintly of chlorine. Its pools are empty; the deepest holds a table, a heater and a dozen locked transport cases. The man at the table rolls a coin across his fingers.
>
> "Debts outlive bodies," he says. "Fortunately, so do opportunities."

Niko has already heard the PCs are awake. He knows it from the broker who sold their sleeves, not from omniscience. He is considering selling that information again. Hale stands near a cracked window and hums four notes while opening a secure case. Familiarity with Juno is available; do not announce the historical identity until a claim is made or verified.

## Niko's opening price

He wants the party to remove his delivery crew's names from a public release while retaining the original ledger in protected evidence custody. He does not demand deletion of evidence about his own decisions if directly challenged; that is his negotiable greed, not an unavoidable quest condition.

Offer three ways to proceed. Agree to protect uninvolved workers and copy the ledger immediately. Offer a specific later favour such as a witnessed statement that the current delivery crew did not know the contents. Or confront him with the recall order showing that the Foundation will seize criminal assets once the witnesses are secured. That last argument gives him a reason to cooperate without payment.

**Diplomacy, Intimidation or Bureaucracy D0**, as appropriate, earns an additional canal access code and his commitment not to sell the route for the rest of the night. On a failed bargaining check he still provides the ledger to protect himself, but retains his ability to sell their departure direction; Heat rises by one unless the party uses another escape. Tell them the consequence first. A good concrete proposal to protect workers can secure the ledger without any roll.

## The ledger

Give [[H04|H04]]. It shows living organic clone substrates delivered to three numbered recipient classes, M9, J4 and A12; a Foundation receiving site; separate invoices for cortical-interface conditioning; and a maintenance delivery entrance at the Spire. The recipient numbers are not publicly labelled AIs. Ordinary clients could use codes. The crucial point at this stage is that the clinic's charity accounts hide elite interface deliveries.

Niko will permit external inspection of one empty transport collar. Its seals verify an organic sleeve shipment; it is not a robotic chassis. A Science or Medicine D0 success identifies that the interface was modified to accommodate a nonstandard continuity architecture. Failure still confirms an organic clone and a specialised interface. The final origin proof is elsewhere.

## Hale's complication

Hale has taken money for confirming that the six stacks were awake. They have not sold the canal escape code and have kept a second vehicle. This is a real betrayal of location, not proof that all affection was false. A player asking why receives an honest answer after a moment of silence.

> "I did not know whether you would come back. I knew the rent would. That is not a defence. It is what I chose."

Hale offers restitution: hand over the payment to Serin's clinic and burn their own broker credential to reduce Heat by one. The party can accept, refuse, ask for different reparations or end the relationship. Do not roll to force forgiveness. Hale's identity can be corroborated by a broker succession record in the transport case and a witnessed archive record at Anansi; an old intimate phrase alone is insufficient.

## An adversary at the waterline

If time and tension justify it, Ren Ko (C17) arrives to buy the same ledger. He snaps disposable gloves and calls Cass "logistics." He offers Niko more money for exclusivity. The PCs can let him bid, record the offer, appeal to Niko's survival interest, or leave by the maintenance stair with their already acquired copy.

No duel is required. Ren retreats if publicly recorded requesting evidence suppression, and the recording becomes extra leverage against Vey. If violence starts, use ADV02 for Ren with one ADV03 assistant; their objective is the physical original, not murdering everyone. If they take it, the copied ledger remains useful but Niko becomes less cooperative.

## What Niko does not know

He does not know the patrons are AIs. He knows prohibited interface cargo has made people rich and other people disappear. He assumes a Meth longevity scheme. If the party articulates the AI theory, he goes still: "Then I have been delivering bodies to the people who decide whether my accounts exist." That earns a genuine reason for him to provide the canal route.

## Exit or compressed version

Hale offers a dry coat and a route to Anansi, without touching anyone. A small gesture can carry the relationship scene. If the lead is skipped, Hale brings a ledger copy to Anansi and admits the location sale there. Niko's worker-protection demand arrives attached. Preserve the moral choice and the evidence, not every line of dialogue.
