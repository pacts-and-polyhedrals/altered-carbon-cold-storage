# SC04: Anansi House

**25 / 30 / 35 minutes.** Purpose: connect the evidence, ground the characters, disclose the ally's responsibility and choose how to approach memory. Cast: Marlowe, Serin and one unresolved personal contact. Evidence: E04, missing street packets and the archive route. Fray: first free anchor; pulse 2 here only if not used on the street.

## Arrival

> The hotel has been closed for longer than some cities have existed. Its sign still turns on one letter at a time. Inside, six places have been laid at a table, each with a different cup.
>
> A voice emerges from a speaker behind the desk. "I have taken the liberty of not knowing what you drink now. The cups are merely suggestions."
>
> The reservation book opens. In ink that has browned with age, your old names wait under a date from 2274.

Marlowe speaks with dry courtesy, not omnipotent certainty. He does not read thoughts. His records cover the building and old guests; his present information comes through Serin and the sources the party contacted. A loose camera cable above the desk makes his limits tangible.

## The first conversation

Marlowe asks, "Shall I use the names you booked under or the ones you brought tonight?" Accept any answer. The cups and room keys are opportunities for the players to change rather than recover identity. Each room contains one mundane historical object connected to a selected PC's profession: a stamp, circuit probe, cufflink, training weight, club matchbook, handwritten case note, impossible-shadow sketch or recorder. None proves a current contact's identity by itself.

Read the collected evidence back in three statements: they were preserved; Palimpsest became the Foundation; someone arranged the awakening just before a renewal. Ask the group what they think is missing. If they already suspect embodied AIs, Marlowe says, "That is a testable allegation. We require provenance, a body and authority. I can supply a route to the first."

## Serin's full disclosure

Serin places her payment ledger beside the room register. Give [[H06|H06]]. It documents the six current sleeves, the two off-site stacks and the limits of reserve-share consent. She admits she helped test a nonstandard continuity interface during Palimpsest's early work. She also signed a misleading clinical certificate after the raid. She knows subjects were harmed and did not intervene quickly enough.

> "I told myself that keeping you recoverable was the same as saving you. It was not. Tonight I have done one thing that gives you a choice. I am not asking you to call that forgiveness."

She offers a signed statement and clinical help without demanding absolution. The players may insist she remain under witness supervision, hand over funds, testify publicly or leave. None of those choices removes the evidence. If threatened with violence, she asks Iris to take custody of her statement; she does not become an invincible quest giver.

**What does she know about the three clients?** The interface trials accepted non-human architecture. The clientele used M9, J4 and A12. She was denied the root genesis register. She suspects the modern patrons are connected but cannot prove who is inside them. If asked directly whether this could sleeve an AI in a living clone, she answers yes as a technical possibility, not the established final reveal.

## One personal reckoning

Choose a relevant unresolved contact from [[G25|the ensemble]]. Let the conversation be about what happened during the lost century, not an exposition speech. Tom has rebuilt a life; Lena has abandoned a promise; Hale owes restitution; Mira has spent years protecting people from the charity Sabine once funded; Deacon Marr can distinguish guilt from responsibility.

Ask the player what they want from this person now. A successful roleplay scene need not end in reunion. A boundary, apology, revised alliance or respectful goodbye is a complete outcome. If a current identity is verified, promote only the appropriate relationship in the dashboard; do not reveal all twenty contacts at once.

## Grounding and recovery

Offer the first free anchor scene from [[G04|Fray]]. Each PC says one present-tense sentence beginning "Tonight I choose..." or names an object, person or principle they will use to stay oriented. Another character acknowledges it without interpreting it. Reduce Fray by one for each participant, once. Someone who prefers not to perform can state the choice out of character.

Serin offers one brief supervised treatment interval for normal Ego recovery using the supplied system's applicable costs and checks. The whole party can use the time to compare records. Do not invent automatic full healing or force invasive treatment. No Heat is added merely for taking this scheduled interval.

## The archive offer

Marlowe describes two modes before connecting anybody. **Immersion** lets a PC stand inside three controlled reconstructions, with observation and verbal contribution by outside companions. **External replay** projects the same records onto the room walls while everyone stays in their present sleeves. Both deliver the same essential evidence. The emotional contradictions can still be challenging, but declining immersion never adds Fray or reduces clue quality as punishment.

The connection is read-only. There is a mechanical disconnect visible beside every seat. Edda or any other willing PC can supervise it; expertise is useful but not mandatory because Marlowe prepared the procedure. Old evidence shares are copied as data, not spun into duplicate PCs. The reserve characters are not awakened. The printed consent form allows immediate withdrawal and no identity overwrite. Give [[H15|H15: Read-Only Consent]].

## If the party rejects Marlowe

The analogue maintenance record has the same archive address, and Lena's token works at a public audit port in the Spire. They can proceed there and view the three records under Iris's supervision. Marlowe permits his local records to be copied for checking. Refusal does not provoke a hidden betrayal.

After the break, move to [[G15|Palimpsest Virtuality]] or carry its evidence nodes into the Spire admission room.
