# H13 - The Three Buses and the Evidence Port

**SPIRE MAINTENANCE DIAGRAM / READ BEFORE IRREVERSIBLE ACTION**

| Control | Function | What it does not do |
|---|---|---|
| Evidence port | Produces a read-only, witness-signed case packet. | Does not require copying a complete living personality. |
| Civic authority lease | Grants, suspends or transfers current root command and delegated tokens. | Does not automatically switch off emergency services. |
| Life-safety bus | Maintains clinics, pumps and emergency routing under qualified operators. | Does not grant those operators permanent political authority. |
| Clinical Palimpsest engine | Performs memory editing and specialised interface maintenance. | Its shutdown does not instantly kill the three currently sleeved residents. |

**Safe handover:** name qualified operators; preserve their access to the life-safety bus; record a limited operations charter; revoke or transfer civic tokens separately; verify service continuity before leaving.

**Clinical isolation:** disconnect current patients and resident minds from write operations; preserve necessary voluntary-care records; quarantine or disable the intended editing functions; document what remains available for accountable treatment.

**Known limits:** off-site backups and research copies are not fully mapped. Local destruction is not proof that every copy of a mind or a procedure has ceased to exist. Civic revocation can still end current administrative power.

Before committing, state the intended evidence outcome, authority outcome and care outcome.
