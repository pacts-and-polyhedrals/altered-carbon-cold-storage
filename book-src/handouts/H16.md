# H16 - What Can Be Chosen Tonight

**Open Circuit:** publish a defensible, victim-redacted case. The truth leaves the room; immediate political control may remain contested.

**The Mortal Charter:** remove or suspend the rulers' authority while preserving personhood, due process and independent care. A real handover and accountable temporary operators are necessary.

**Cut the Thread:** isolate and destroy or quarantine the coercive editing engine. Existing victims are not instantly repaired; valuable voluntary treatment may need to be preserved separately.

**The Witness Compact:** exchange limited publication or delay for enforceable protections, outside oversight and a fixed review. The compact needs independent evidence custody and consequences for breach.

**A Throne With New Names:** transfer the apparatus to a new authority. The power becomes yours or your allies'; its dangers do not disappear. Decide who can remove the new operators.

**Carry the Embers:** leave with the witnesses, reserves and evidence you can protect. Survival is a real outcome. The city may remain under its present rulers.

These can be combined. State three things: **what happens to the evidence, who holds authority, and how people receive care**. No collective choice authorises overwriting another person's mind.

A local body, a recoverable mind and governing authority are separate things. Decide which you are acting upon.
