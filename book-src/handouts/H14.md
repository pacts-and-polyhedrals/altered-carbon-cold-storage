# H14 - Three Places to Start

**PAPER - THE QUIET ARCHIVE**

Father Ash preserves old custody records in a dry chapel north of the Night Stair market. Ask about the 2274 order and the trust that inherited its obligations. He will not require you to share his faith. He will ask you to respect other people's consent.

**CARGO - BLUE LOTUS**

Niko Saint works beneath the shuttered bathhouse west of the market. He keeps delivery records because records are leverage. Ask what the Foundation has been receiving through elite-client accounts. Assume he will want something specific, not necessarily money.

**RELAYS - SABLE NEEDLE**

Lena Orr is at Relay Seventeen above the south canal. She has old technical records and reasons to fear what using them will cost. Ask about migrated Palimpsest domains and persistent client certificates. Decide whether you are willing to protect the person who provides the evidence.

**SHELTER - ANANSI HOUSE**

The old hotel inland still answers its hardline. Your former group used it. Dr Serin Kael has arranged for missing source packets and a safe place to compare them there. You can go there first.
