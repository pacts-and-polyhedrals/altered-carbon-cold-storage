# H15 - Read-Only Archive Agreement

I choose whether to enter an immersive reconstruction or view the same evidence externally in my current sleeve. Both modes permit questions, pauses and withdrawal.

The archive may read the specific case share I authorise. It may not copy my complete personality, create an active duplicate, alter my present memories, select a dominant identity, perform treatment or connect a bidirectional clinical channel without a separate explicit agreement.

The mechanical disconnect remains accessible. Ending a viewing session does not surrender access to already obtained evidence. External records and witnesses remain available.

A previously recorded permission to release case data does not authorise awakening a stored person. The two reserve continuities remain protected unless a separate valid decision changes that.

I may ask another participant to monitor the connection. I may decline a description, a personal prompt or the entire procedure. No explanation of a personal boundary is required.

**Chosen mode:** immersion / external replay / neither.

**Chosen name and witness:** to be recorded by the participants, not inferred from a sleeve identifier.

Marlowe's addition: "A reservation is an invitation to stay. It is not a lock on the door."
