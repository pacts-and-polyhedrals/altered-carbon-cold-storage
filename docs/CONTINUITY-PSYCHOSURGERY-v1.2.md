## Core 2020: no automatic sleeve-count check

The number of prior bodies is contextual. Event-specific rules determine the loss: compelled *cross-sleeving* needs qualifying criteria (including the DHF age rule and exceptions); synthetic quality downgrades use printed comparisons; deaths and some Virtual experiences can cause recoverable or permanent EP loss. Chronologically inert stack storage is not equivalent to 110 subjective years in an incarceration simulator. Do **not** retrospectively replay historical death rolls from all four–seven previous lives.

The Core printed Baggage choices are:

| Official Baggage | Choice A | Choice B |
|---|---|---|
| Trauma | Downgrade **two Willpower Skills** one level | Lose **EP2d6** |
| Personality Frag | Downgrade **two Acuity Skills** one level | Lose **EP2d6** |
| Compromised DHF | Downgrade **two Intelligence Skills** one level | Lose **EP2d6** |

The two Skill records must be distinct and have a level high enough to be downgraded. Only one branch may be selected per Baggage Item. Since 1.3.0 the console resolves the choice through the Altered Carbon 2.4 Baggage workflow, which marks the Baggage Item itself as resolved; the character sheet and console both honour that record (and the older hidden Actor marker), so a second charge is impossible from either place. It requires the GM and player to affirm **the penalty is not already reflected in imported starting statistics**. Printed Baggage is not the same as the adventure Fray track, and the EP alternative is normally recoverable unless the relevant official effect says otherwise.

## Optional house-rule Fray (0–6)

At each of these **five** events, GM and player choose how to respond: *Ward dislocation*, *first verified historical-contact contradiction*, *Court memory node*, *Last Broadcast memory node*, and *core's three-voice challenge*. Only one use of each per PC.

- **Stay present**: Composure in realspace or Discipline in Virtual. Difficulty D0 at Fray 0–3, D1 at Fray 4–5. Success: no increase. Failure: +1 Fray.
- **Accept an echo**: voluntarily +1 Fray, gaining a true extra detail or minor negotiated concession. It may never dictate the PC's actions.
- **Veil or decline**: no check or penalty, and no essential clue is withheld.
- **Anchors**: one once-only supportive grounding opportunity at Anansi House and another before the finale; each -1 Fray. Does not repair Ego.
- **Overreach**: voluntary, informed +2 Fray and **raw** EP1d6 (reduce with Core WB etc), at most once per PC per scene, in return for a concrete optional advantage. Not needed to obtain any essential clue.
- **At Fray 6**: ask the player for their own meaningful choice. *Integration* resets to 3 with player-authored identity/relationship change; *Partition* resets to 4 and costs raw EP1d6 and locks out further overreach for the night; *Shelter* once only resets to 4 with Heat +1 or clear narrative concession if Heat is already capped. No spontaneous permanent Ego loss, compulsory violence, diagnosis or seizure of PC control is implied.
- **Opt-out**: mark the PC exempt; the adventure proceeds with no Fray cost or punishment for using a safety boundary.

Fray uses GM-only world setting state for pulse use, anchor use, consent decisions and treatment notes, alongside the existing GM Book's saved per-PC Fray values. All committed outcomes are logged. Re-running a resolved pulse or starting Baggage penalty is prohibited.

## Severe Core continuity crises

Keep three cases distinct: (1) a **Personality Frag Baggage** starting consequence; (2) **Ego Splinter** or possible Trauma Loop/Personality Frag when an Ego collapses or its irreversible loss exceeds intact remaining EP; and (3) the **Interrogation Personality Frag** triggered effect, which requires +++++ (or +++ using Sharya Protocols). The Core treats the latter as an exceptionally severe event, not something produced by meeting a historical loved one.

The console displays current EP, permanent loss, effective recoverable maximum and a warning at zero EP or when permanent loss exceeds remaining EP. The rulebook allows player-directed continuation as a substantially changed DHF after treatment. The GM must determine the actual implications with that player.

## Psychosurgery and clinical treatment

- Enter Virtual first. A treatment requires the appropriate software and Medicine / Read Person / Data Engineering roll. The **parent Altered Carbon v2.4.0 Clinical Console** handles its own published roll outcomes; this adventure does not simulate it twice.
- A standard + assigned to *Psychosurgery* restores EP1d4, with permitted software-tier upgrades. At +++++, *Ego Reconstruction* may restore EP2d4 plus the conditional Specialisation choice. Negative Feedback and Trauma Loop have severe harmful consequences.
- SP5 may be spent to regain EP1d6 + WB per Core p.147 (subject to official limits). The parent system handles available resource maximum and permanent loss.
- A treatment note in Cold Storage records the session and the character's current Ego, but **never changes Ego or Fray**.
- The original adventure offers one potential brief treatment interval with Serin at Anansi House, subject to normal risks and costs. It does not guarantee that an ongoing personality crisis is reversible during a four-hour game.

## Old-world migration: data safety

**Book Only** is the safe starting choice. It updates 96 source-managed journal pages, preserves deliberate handout grants and existing custom scene links, and creates a GM-only recovery journal when a managed page is replaced.

**Continuity → Review World** previews an opt-in *additive migration*: add missing new prelude Sleeve Items, mark two contested archives, and update the original Sabine active sleeve classification **only if the source-controlled initial sleeve still remains active**. If a PC has changed bodies, that PC is skipped completely. It never resets EP/SP/HP, Skill levels, personal gear, relationship status, user ownership, custom scenes or journal disclosure. No Baggage consequences are charged automatically. A failed individual document is reported; successful additions stay and the update can be retried without duplicates. **Back up first.**

**Full Import** is for a fresh world only and now refuses a second attempt if original pregen Actors already exist or an installed flag is recorded, preventing accidental replacement of played statistics.
